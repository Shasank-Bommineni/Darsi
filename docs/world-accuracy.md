# World accuracy

What in this game is Darsi, and what is a plausible stand-in for Darsi.

Every element is labelled with one of:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **REAL DATA** | Taken directly from a survey source (OSM / SRTM). Position and shape are measured. |
| **REFERENCE-BASED** | Form follows the documented regional type; this specific instance was not surveyed. |
| **PROCEDURAL** | Generated by this project's code. Plausible, deterministic, not a record of anything. |
| **APPROXIMATED** | Derived from real data by inference, so partly measured and partly assumed. |
| **NOT YET AVAILABLE** | Should exist in the real town; we have no data and have not faked it. |

Current world build: **1,040 road edges / 137.55 km**, **7,254 buildings**,
**45 POIs**, terrain 74.1–317.7 m, 8.4 × 8.4 km.

---

## Terrain

| Element | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Elevation surface | **REAL DATA** | NASA SRTM 1-arcsec, 769² grid at 10.94 m spacing. Anchor ≈112 m, range 74.1–317.7 m. Darsi is not flat and the game does not pretend it is. |
| Road corridor grading | **APPROXIMATED** | Centrelines are smoothed and the longitudinal gradient is clamped (5.5% on highway/arterial, 8.5% elsewhere), then blended 6–10 m into the surrounding ground. Real roads are graded; SRTM at 30 m posting does not resolve that, so we reconstruct it. Steepest gradient in the shipped build: **8.5%**. |
| Water body beds | **APPROXIMATED** | Tank and pond outlines are REAL DATA; the dished bed (0.8–2.6 m deep) and water level are inferred from the outline and surrounding terrain. |
| Land cover (field / scrub / dry earth / town / grass) | **APPROXIMATED** | Painted from OSM `landuse` and `natural` polygons where they exist, and inferred from the urbanity field where they do not. |
| Micro-relief, soil colour variation | **PROCEDURAL** | Deterministic noise on top of the measured surface. |

## Roads

| Element | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Road geometry and topology | **REAL DATA** | Every carriageway in the game is a surveyed OSM way, clipped to the world square, welded and re-tessellated but never invented. |
| Road names | **REAL DATA** | e.g. *Podili - Vinukonda Road*, *MDR087*. Unnamed ways stay unnamed. |
| Road classification | **APPROXIMATED** | OSM `highway=*` mapped onto game classes (arterial 129, major 40, secondary 96, townroad 311, residential 349, lane 112, farm 3). |
| Carriageway width, shoulders, lane count | **APPROXIMATED** | Taken from `width`/`lanes` tags where present; otherwise from the class, following IRC practice for Indian rural and small-town roads. |
| Surface type (asphalt / concrete / gravel / dirt) | **APPROXIMATED** | From the OSM `surface` tag when tagged; otherwise inferred from class and urbanity. |
| Potholes, patches, edge break-up, crown camber | **PROCEDURAL** | Geometric features — real depressions in the mesh, not decals — seeded per edge and weighted by a quality score. You feel them because the physics samples the *same* function that built the mesh (`surfaceOffset()` in `roadBuilder.js`). Their individual positions are invented. |
| Lane markings | **APPROXIMATED** | Drawn where the class and quality make them plausible; fading markings on poorer roads. |
| Speed breakers | **REAL DATA where mapped** | From `traffic_calming` nodes. The current extract contains **none**, so none are placed. |

## Buildings

| Element | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 142 mapped footprints | **REAL DATA** | Used verbatim, `src="osm"` in the data. Includes building names where OSM has them. |
| Large mapped sites (>2,200 m²) | **APPROXIMATED** | One such way exists — *Institute of Driver Training and Research*, 1.7 ha. OSM traces the whole campus as a single `building` way. Extruding that would put a 193 m box in the landscape, so it is rendered as a walled compound with a gate, a name board and generated blocks inside. Boundary REAL DATA; contents PROCEDURAL. |
| 7,085 generated buildings | **PROCEDURAL** | Placed only inside the measured built-up envelope, always facing a surveyed street, never overlapping a real footprint, a carriageway or water, and rejected where the ground varies more than 3.4 m across the plot. **These are not records of real houses.** |
| Building typology | **REFERENCE-BASED** | Categories and their proportions follow the fabric of a Prakasam-district mandal headquarters: flat-roofed RCC houses with parapets and terrace water tanks, shophouses with roll-down shutters and signboards, tin-roofed sheds, unfinished buildings with rebar stubs, a few 3–5 storey blocks in the centre. |
| Heights | **APPROXIMATED** | OSM `building:levels`/`height` are respected when tagged. Otherwise storeys come from category and urbanity (1:2,808 2:3,707 3:619 4:70 5:49), with per-building floor-height variation — mean 5.94 m, standard deviation 2.47 m. No two neighbours are identical. |
| Colours, plaster, trim, signage text | **PROCEDURAL** | Deterministic per building. Shop names on signboards are generic Indian retail names, not real businesses. |

## Landmarks and religious buildings

| Element | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| *Venkateswara swamy devalayamu* | **REAL DATA (position + name)**, **REFERENCE-BASED (form)** | The only place of worship in the OSM extract. It stands where OSM says it stands and keeps its real name; the structure itself is a generated Dravidian-style temple, because we have no survey of the actual building. |
| 26 neighbourhood temples | **PROCEDURAL** | A mandal headquarters without a visible temple would be a worse falsehood than an honestly-labelled generated one. These are placed near settlement centres, are **deliberately unnamed**, and represent "a temple of this kind is here", not a specific temple. |
| Mosques, churches | **NOT YET AVAILABLE** | Builders exist in code (`buildMosque`, `buildChurch`) but no OSM record places one, so none are generated. |
| Bus stations | **REAL DATA (position)**, **PROCEDURAL (structure)** | Two mapped; shelters are generated. |
| Any other named landmark | **NOT YET AVAILABLE** | Nothing is given a real name unless OSM provides it. |

## Infrastructure and vegetation

| Element | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Utility poles, cross-arms, overhead wires | **PROCEDURAL** | Laid out along surveyed roads at class-dependent spacing; wires hang in real catenaries between consecutive poles. |
| Street lighting | **PROCEDURAL** | On the busier urban roads only; switches on after dusk. |
| Transformers, open drains, kerbs | **PROCEDURAL** | Density follows the urbanity field. |
| Compound walls and gates | **PROCEDURAL** | Mapped OSM barriers would be REAL DATA, but the extract contains **none**. |
| High-tension power lines (3) and railways (17 ways) | **REAL DATA (geometry)** | Present in the data; pylon/track geometry is not yet rendered — **NOT YET AVAILABLE** visually. |
| Trees and shrubs | **REFERENCE-BASED (species mix)**, **PROCEDURAL (positions)** | Neem, tamarind, banyan, palmyra, coconut and babul, mixed by land cover: avenue planting near streets, scrub on dry ground, palmyra in the fields. No individual tree is a real tree. |
| Crops | **REFERENCE-BASED** | Field-scale planting inside mapped farmland, with per-field uniformity and fallow plots. |

## Traffic and people

| Element | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle mix | **REFERENCE-BASED** | Motorcycles and scooters dominate, with autos, cars, buses, trucks, tractors and bicycles weighted per road class. |
| Traffic behaviour | **PROCEDURAL** | Drives the real road graph, keeps left, yields, follows, horns. Not a record of real traffic. |
| Pedestrians | **PROCEDURAL** | Numbers scale with the urbanity field and the clock — busy 08:00–11:00 and 17:00–20:30, nearly empty at 03:00. |

## Environment

| Element | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sun path | **APPROXIMATED** | Computed for latitude 15.77 N, so the sun passes nearly overhead at noon, as it does in Prakasam. |
| Time of day, light colour | **REFERENCE-BASED** | Twelve keyframes across the day. |
| Weather states | **PROCEDURAL** | Clear / hazy / cloudy / overcast / rain, each with real consequences: fog density, light level, visible rain streaks, wet-darkened road materials, reduced grip, lightning and thunder. Not tied to real Darsi meteorology. |
| Audio | **PROCEDURAL** | Everything is synthesised at run time — engine, tyres, wind, horns, birds, insects, rain, thunder. No recordings. |

## The motorcycle

| Element | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| "Sahaja 125" | **REFERENCE-BASED, original design** | Not a copy of any manufacturer's model. Dimensions are those of the Indian 125cc commuter class: 118 kg kerb, 1.285 m wheelbase, 0.635 m wheels, 0.79 m seat height, 0.70 m wide, 1.98 m long. |
| Engine and gearbox | **REFERENCE-BASED** | 10.8 Nm at 6,500 rpm, 9,200 rpm redline, 4-speed, 56 km/l, 11 l tank. Measured in the test suite: **87 km/h top speed, 0–60 km/h in 5.4 s, 22.6 m to stop from 55 km/h.** |

## Known gaps

Honest list of what is missing, not a to-do list disguised as a feature list.

- **Interiors.** No building can be entered.
- **Mosques and churches.** Code exists; no data to place them.
- **Railway and HT-line structures.** Geometry is loaded but not rendered.
- **Mapped barriers, speed breakers, street-level POI detail.** The OSM extract
  for Darsi simply does not contain them. Where there is no data, there is
  nothing in the game, rather than something invented and presented as real.
- **Named businesses.** Signboards carry generic names. The real shops of Darsi
  are not in OSM and we are not going to guess them.
- **Building appearance of the 142 real footprints.** Their *outlines* are
  surveyed; their facades, colours and roofs are generated like every other
  building, because no imagery of them may lawfully be used as source material.
